💉 FluShot

Encrypted sample vault. Encrypt fetches samples straight to ciphertext so AV never sees them; Decrypt restores them for analysis (run that inside your VM).

📁
Encrypted blobs are saved to
FluShot/vault/
The key file .flushot.key lives here too — copy it into your VM's vault once so Decrypt can work there.
